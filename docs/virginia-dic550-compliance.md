# Virginia DIC 550 — Compliance Tracker

**Source:** DIC 550 (06/01/2020) — *Curriculum Vendor Agreement, Computer-Based Training, Driver Improvement Clinic Program*, Commonwealth of Virginia. Authority: Va. Code § 46.2-490 et seq.
**Status:** Road Ready Safety is currently an **affiliate/referral partner** (operating under the approved provider's license), **not** an approved Virginia curriculum vendor. Most of DIC 550 binds the *approved vendor*, not an affiliate — so it becomes our obligation only when we pursue our own approval.
**This doc tracks two things:** (A) what the *public website* must do, and (B) the full provider-approval roadmap (course + company + ops).

> ⚠️ Not legal advice. Verify corporate-registration and advertising questions with counsel, and verify all VA program facts against current DMV guidance before the page goes public.

## B. PROVIDER-APPROVAL roadmap (course + company + ops — NOT website work)

### B1. Company / legal
- [ ] Register as a Virginia corporation, or a foreign corporation authorized to transact business in VA; identify registered office + registered agent — **§2.5, §2.14**
- [ ] Decide whether RRS will own/operate clinic(s); if so, submit signed **Form DI 551** (Online Driver Improvement Clinic Agreement) — **§2.12, §2.13**
- [ ] Do not own/operate a clinic before obtaining DMV permission — **§2.13**
- [ ] Provide signed/dated DIC 550 + current curriculum at signing; submit any new version ≥30 days before use — **§5.11, §5.13**

### B2. Course / curriculum
- [ ] Course **≥ 8 hours including the final exam** — **§3.2**
- [ ] Cover all required topics **a–u** (safe driving, the driver, fuel-efficient driving, pre-trip inspections, organ donation, alcohol/drugs, signs, driving conditions, passing/turning/lanes/intersections, collisions, occupant protection, road emergencies, city/suburban/rural/expressway, aggressive driving, truck safety, motorcycle awareness, pedestrian safety, bicycle safety, handicapped parking, distracted driving, licensing/registration) — **§3.2**
- [ ] **Sixth-grade reading & comprehension level** — **§2.9**
- [ ] Materials free of race/ethnic/gender stereotypes — **§2.6**
- [ ] Clearly define purpose, goals, objectives — **§2.8**
- [ ] Use **current Virginia statistics** wherever stats appear — **§3.2**
- [ ] Graphics/sound/color + visual/auditory effects that aid instruction & engagement — **§3.3**
- [ ] Required interaction; quizzes/tests with **≥3 multiple-choice answers**; corrective, timely feedback; ability to review completed modules — **§3.4**
- [ ] Course can be taken in increments; designed for minimal keyboarding/computer skill — **§3.5**
- [ ] Provide research/validity materials showing rehabilitation value — **§2.4**

### B3. Identity verification & testing
- [ ] Procedures to verify the test-taker is the enrolled student — **§2.11**
- [ ] **Prohibited:** virtual e-learning and facial recognition as identity-verification methods — **§2.7**

### B4. Help desk / support (ops)
- [ ] Toll-free phone access to a person and/or online responses; assistance with technical content — **§3.6**
- [ ] Posted weekday hours (and weekend if DMV requires) by mutual agreement — **§3.6**

### B5. Accessibility (ADA)
- [ ] Course media fully accessible to hearing-impaired and others needing accommodation — **§2.10, §3.1**

### B6. Deliverables to DMV (for approval)
- [ ] Curriculum description; help-desk training outline; all quiz/test questions + answer guide; identity-validation documentation; paper copy of course; evaluation access for DMV; accessible media — **§3.1**

### B7. Audit & ongoing
- [ ] Cooperate with DMV audits (incl. undisclosed testers); 30-day window to cure non-compliance — **§4.1–§4.4**
- [ ] No membership fee charged to clinics using the curriculum — **§3.7**
