## 2.2 Student identity verification

Our courses are approved on the condition that the person who enrolls is the person who completes the course and receives the certificate.

- **At enrollment** you provide your full legal name, date of birth, driver license number and issuing state, email address and mailing address, and you affirm that this information is true and that you alone will complete the course.
- **At every login** you use your registered email and password and then enter a time-sensitive code sent to your registered email.
- **During the course** all activity is tied to your authenticated account. Course timers, progression controls and completion tracking follow that identity.
- **Where the approving state requires it**, the course also presents identity questions at set intervals or before the final exam. The course page for that state describes the schedule and what happens if a question is answered incorrectly.

If we determine in our reasonable judgment that identity verification has failed or that someone other than the enrolled student is participating, we may suspend or terminate access, record a failing status and decline to issue a certificate. Re-enrollment after an identity-related termination is a new registration at full price.

# Part 3. State-specific provisions

## 3.1 Texas driving safety course (TDLR)

- **Provider.** {{legalName}} d/b/a {{dba}} is licensed by the Texas Department of Licensing and Regulation as a driving safety course provider. The provider name and license number appear on the Texas course pages, the enrollment contract and the certificate.
- **Cancellation and refunds (Texas Education Code §1001.401 and §1001.402).** You may cancel for a full refund before midnight of the third calendar day after signing the enrollment contract, excluding weekends and legal holidays, even if you have accessed the course, unless you have completed the course or failed its quizzes within that period. After the third day, a student who withdraws before completing all instructional hours receives a refund of the unused portion of tuition for the hours not provided; we may retain not more than $50 as an administrative expense. If we discontinue the course or a material failure on our part prevents completion, everything you paid is refundable (§1001.403). Refunds are paid within thirty (30) days of the effective date of withdrawal. The standard administrative fee, currently $3.00, is non-refundable once registration is complete. Full text: the [Texas refund policy](/texas/refund).
- **Completion deadline.** All course requirements must be completed within ninety (90) days of registration unless a court sets a shorter deadline. The course consists of 300 minutes of instruction plus required breaks.
- **Complaints.** Texas Department of Licensing and Regulation, Driver Education and Safety, P.O. Box 12157, Austin, Texas 78711; fax (512) 463-9468; online at [tdlr.texas.gov/help](https://www.tdlr.texas.gov/help/); telephone 800-803-9202 or (512) 463-6599. This information also appears on the certificate of completion. You are not required to use our grievance process before contacting TDLR.
- **Records.** Records for students who completed within the previous twelve months are accessible at our primary place of business; older records are retrievable from secure storage on request. Instructor and staff qualification records are kept for at least five (5) years.

## 3.2 Ohio adult remedial driving course (Ohio Department of Public Safety)

- **Provider.** The Ohio course is offered by {{legalName}} d/b/a {{dba}} under Ohio Revised Code 4510.037 and 4510.038 and Ohio Administrative Code Chapter 4501-21. Its approval status and, once issued, its enterprise number appear on the Ohio course pages.
- **Completion window.** Ohio requires every student to finish the complete eight hours of training within thirty (30) days of the date and time the student first starts the course content. After thirty days the student must begin the course again (OAC 4501-21-14).
- **Final examination.** Forty questions, drawn from a state-required question bank, passed at 75 percent. A student who fails may retake the examination once, no sooner than 24 hours later. A student who fails twice must retake the course (OAC 4501-21-13(C)(8)).
- **Identity validation.** In addition to Part 2.2, the Ohio course presents an identity question after every two hours of credited instruction and before the final examination. An incorrect answer allows one more attempt; a second incorrect answer locks the course for 24 hours.
- **Instruction time.** Ohio credits only measured instruction time. Break time is recorded and does not count toward the eight hours.
- **What the course does.** Completing the course may support a two-point credit for a driver with at least two but fewer than twelve points (once every three years, five times in a lifetime), and it is one of the reinstatement requirements after a twelve-point suspension. Completion does not remove points from a driving record. A course taken under a court order does not earn a credit.
- **Complaints.** Ohio Department of Public Safety, Driver Training Program Office, 1970 West Broad Street, Columbus, Ohio 43223; drivertraining@dps.ohio.gov; (614) 466-3524.

## 3.3 North Dakota defensive driving course (NDDOT)

- **What the course does.** An approved course may support a three-point reduction once every twelve months, capped at nine points in any three-year period, and may be elected at court in lieu of points for violations carrying five points or fewer (NDCC 39-06.1-13 and 39-06.1-10.1).
- **Reporting.** The student delivers the certificate of completion to the North Dakota Department of Transportation, Driver License Division (drs@nd.gov). A course elected in lieu of points must be reported within thirty (30) days of the election.
- **Complaints.** North Dakota Department of Transportation, Driver License Division, 608 East Boulevard Avenue, Bismarck, ND 58505-0700; (701) 328-2600.

## 3.4 Idaho defensive driving course (Idaho Transportation Department)

- **What the course does.** Completion of an approved course may remove three points from an Idaho driving record once every three years (IDAPA 39.02.71). Any citation must be paid before the reduction applies. Drivers 55 and older may qualify for an insurance discount at the insurer's discretion.
- **Reporting.** The student submits the certificate to the Idaho Transportation Department, DMV, PO Box 7129, Boise, ID 83707-1129, or ddc@itd.idaho.gov.
- **Complaints.** Idaho Transportation Department, Driver Services; (208) 334-8735.

## 3.5 Missouri Driver Improvement Program (Missouri Safety Center)

- **What the course does.** When a court or the Fine Collection Center authorizes a driver to complete an approved Driver Improvement Program, the points for that violation are not assessed. The course must be completed within sixty (60) days of the plea or conviction and may be used for this purpose once in any thirty-six (36) month period (RSMo 302.302). The court decides whether to authorize the program.
- **Reporting.** The completion form is sent to the Missouri Department of Revenue, Driver License Bureau, PO Box 200, Jefferson City, MO 65105-0200, by the student or by us where the court permits, and to the court if instructed.
- **Complaints.** Missouri Safety Center, University of Central Missouri, Warrensburg, MO 64093; (660) 543-4830.
